## 2. Rationale Behind the MIT License

The decision to use the MIT License is based on the following principles:

1. **Simplicity**
 The license is concise, easy to understand, and avoids unnecessary legal complexity. It provides clear permissions without ambiguous clauses.

2. **Freedom of Use**
 It allows anyone to use *kaimose* freely — for personal, educational, or commercial purposes — while maintaining credit to the author.

3. **Encouragement of Collaboration**
 By removing unnecessary restrictions, the license promotes adoption, modification, and contribution from others if the project becomes public in the future.

4. **Compatibility**
 The MIT License is widely compatible with both open-source and proprietary codebases, ensuring that *kaimose* can integrate with a broad range of tools and libraries without legal friction.

5. **Ownership Preservation**
 Although the license is permissive, it preserves the author’s intellectual ownership.
 The copyright remains explicitly attributed to **Adrián Castro Vázquez**.

## 3. Permissions Granted

Under the MIT License, users are explicitly permitted to:

- Use the software for any purpose, including commercial.
- Copy and distribute the code in source or binary form.
- Modify and create derivative works.
- Include the software as part of larger applications.
- Sell software that incorporates *kaimose* code.

These actions are allowed **without needing explicit permission from the author**, as long as the license text and copyright notice remain intact.

While permissive, the MIT License includes important conditions:

## 6. Relationship with Contributions

If external contributions are accepted in the future, all contributors agree that:

- Their submissions are licensed under the same MIT License.
- Contributions are made voluntarily and without expectation of compensation.
- The project maintainer reserves the right to review, modify, or reject contributions to ensure technical and legal consistency.

---

## 7. Future Licensing Considerations

At present, the MIT License fully covers all code and documentation under *kaimose*.
If future modules, plugins, or integrations require different terms (e.g., dual licensing or proprietary extensions), those cases will be explicitly documented and isolated.

Any change to the project’s licensing model will be recorded as a formal **Architectural Decision Record (ADR)** under `DOCS/ADR/`.
